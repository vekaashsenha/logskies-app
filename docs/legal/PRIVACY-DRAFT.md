## 1. Scope

This policy explains how LogSkies handles information in its website, connected workspace and Android application. The public interactive demo stores its working records in the user's browser; the connected workspace stores organization records with our service providers. This policy describes those differences rather than treating all data as local.

## 2. Information processed

Account information includes your email, authentication identifiers and session information. Authentication is handled by Supabase. Passwords are submitted to the authentication service; they must not be included in support requests or operational reports.

Organization and fleet records can include organization name/logo, membership roles, drone names and UINs, battery asset tags, chemistry, capacity, cell count and preflight timestamps/associations. Imported flight records may contain precise coordinates, timestamps, aircraft identifiers, voltage/current measurements, original binary logs and source-file hashes. Reviews may contain pilot names and RPC details, permission references, mission purpose, incidents and reviewer identity. Do not upload personal information that is unnecessary for the operational record.

Our hosting, authentication and email providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser/device information and security logs. We do not operate advertising tracking in the current application code. Contact email, once activated, will process messages and attachments you choose to send.

## 3. Why information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide organization access, store and synchronize fleet/preflight/flight records, identify equipment, create reviewable reports, send account confirmation and recovery emails, handle support and privacy requests, and investigate security incidents. We do not use uploaded flight logs for advertising or sell those logs. New uses will require an updated notice and any choice or consent required by applicable law.

## 4. Sharing and service providers

Authorized members can access their organization's records according to their role. Reports or originals you download and send outside LogSkies are controlled by your sharing choices.

Supabase provides authentication, database and private file storage; the current project is hosted in Tokyo, Japan. Cloudflare hosts the public web application and handles DNS. Resend delivers transactional authentication emails. Expo provides Android build/distribution infrastructure; remote app updates, if configured later, will be reflected in this notice. Providers may process technical data in locations outside India according to their services. We may disclose information where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect lawful rights, with safeguards and notice where permitted. No claim of India-only hosting is made.

## 5. Device permissions and local storage

Android camera access is requested to scan battery QR labels. The current QR workflow does not upload camera photographs or record audio. Manual battery selection is available. Deny or revoke camera access through Android settings if you do not want to use scanning. Flight coordinates are obtained from uploaded telemetry; the current app does not request continuous background phone location.

Web authentication and the demo use browser storage; demo logs and evidence use IndexedDB. Android persists account/session information through its configured local storage. Signing out clears the active application view/session but is not deletion of hosted organization records. Clearing local browser/app data can remove locally held demo information.

## 6. Security

Organization membership policies and private storage restrict hosted record access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your email account, credentials, device and exported records. Send privacy/security concerns through the verified contact channel; do not send passwords or reset links. We will handle incident communications in accordance with applicable requirements.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Records are retained while needed to provide the organization workspace and meet applicable obligations. Exact retention periods, request processing targets and backup expiry must be approved and implemented before this policy is finalized. This draft intentionally does not promise an untested 30-day deletion or backup purge.

Once the contact channel is active, users can request access, correction, export, withdrawal of consent where relevant, or account deletion. We will verify identity proportionately. Requests involving organization-owned operational records may require an authorized organization's involvement. Explain any lawful exception or retained record to the requester. A public self-service account-deletion mechanism and Play Store deletion URL still need implementation; signing out is not that mechanism.

## 8. Children and policy changes

The proposed service eligibility is adults aged 18 or older; it is not intended for children. Confirm the eligibility rule before publication. We will date policy changes and provide appropriate notice for material changes. Do not claim compliance with every data-protection regime solely because this document exists.
