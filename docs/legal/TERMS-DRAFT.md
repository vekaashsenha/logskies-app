## 4. Flight safety and reporting limitations

You remain responsible for aircraft registration, pilot qualifications, applicable operational permissions, safe operation, battery inspection and compliance with law. A QR scan identifies an asset; a preflight record does not confirm takeoff. Parsed arming intervals may differ from actual takeoff and landing. Missing or inaccurate telemetry can affect outputs. Battery-health estimates are review aids and do not certify airworthiness or authorize flight or retirement.

LogSkies is independent of DGCA and eGCA. Reports are Flight Operations Reports with evidence status, not DGCA-approved forms or guaranteed audit acceptance. Check the receiving authority's requirements and review the complete record before relying on or submitting it. The service does not grant airspace permission or replace official operational information.
